The concept

Two ways to get to your next wave

Organised in person

Tell a TAsF surf guide what you want and we organise the trip with you: destination, dates, flights, where to stay, boards, coaching and the plan for flat days.

Planned with AI

Answer a few questions and the world map lights up with the places that work for you. You get a top 5, a 7-day surf forecast, a sample itinerary and ready-made flight and stay searches.
